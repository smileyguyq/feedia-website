What it does

Food first. Personal by design.

Most restaurant discovery starts with a place. incrEdible starts with the food itself, then layers in the individual preferences that make a recommendation useful to a specific person.

  1. Discover the dish.

    Browse and discover individual food items instead of treating every restaurant as one undifferentiated result.

    →

  2. Teach it your taste.

    Simple Dislike, Like, and Love reactions help shape what incrEdible understands about what you enjoy.

    →

  3. Fit results to your needs.

    incrEdible Fit is designed to keep dietary needs visible while people discover and compare food.

    →

  4. See your match.

    Personalized match information helps put a recommendation in the context of your own taste.

The problem

“Best” is not the same as “best for me.”

Restaurant ratings are useful, but they compress thousands of different preferences into one average. A highly rated dish can still be completely wrong for the person choosing dinner.

incrEdible is designed around a different idea: discovery becomes more useful when the software can understand the person making the choice.

Start with intent.

What kind of food sounds good right now?

Add personal context.

What does this person tend to enjoy, avoid, or need?

Make the choice easier.

Surface food that is more likely to fit the person and the moment.

Bite Me

For the “just pick something” moment.

Bite Me is incrEdible’s decision feature for the times when someone does not want another list to scroll through. It is designed to narrow the moment down to a single recommendation using personal taste, distance, and price context.

The goal is not more choice. It is a better decision with less work.
