About us.

We’re a software company based in Utah. We build consumer apps around the people who use them.

Inspired by finding the best chili cheese dog — and discovering nothing really could.

incrEdible started with a surprisingly simple question: Who has the best chili cheese dog near me? The internet had plenty of restaurant ratings, reviews, maps, and menus, but none of them could really answer it. They could tell us whether people liked a restaurant. They couldn’t reliably tell us whether they liked the particular thing we wanted to eat.

That question turned into another: Why are we still discovering restaurants when what we’re actually looking for is food?

From there, incrEdible began to take shape. Individual dishes became the starting point. Then came a bigger realization: even knowing which chili cheese dog everyone else likes doesn’t necessarily tell you which one you will like. Taste is personal. Dietary needs are personal. Distance, price, who you’re eating with, and what sounds good right now all matter.

So we kept building.

incrEdible grew into a different kind of food-discovery experience — one designed to learn what you like, help you discover individual dishes, put recommendations in the context of your own Taste, account for food compatibility, and even help answer the dreaded “What do I want to eat?” question when you don’t feel like searching at all.

That journey also became the foundation for Feedia LLC.

We approach software a little differently. We believe people shouldn’t have to learn how software thinks. Software should learn how people think. The difficult work belongs underneath the experience, not in front of the person using it. Features shouldn’t exist simply because they can; they should work together to make the next decision easier.

That philosophy shapes everything we build. We start with the problem, question the assumptions around how it has traditionally been solved, and keep working until the experience feels natural. We would rather build something compelling that people genuinely want to use than rush out something technically functional and call it finished.

We don’t write software.
We dream it.

And sometimes, apparently, it starts with a $3.29 chili cheese dog.
