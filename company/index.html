Feedia LLC

Software around people.

Feedia is a Utah software company focused on building consumer experiences that anticipate what people need instead of asking people to adapt to the software.

What we believe

People shouldn't have to learn to use software.

Technology can be powerful without feeling complicated. Feedia’s approach is to do the difficult thinking underneath the experience so the software itself can feel more intuitive, adaptive, and natural.

What we build

Products, not complexity.

Feedia develops consumer software. Our current product, incrEdible, applies that philosophy to food discovery by combining item-level discovery with personal taste and food compatibility.
